THE PROBLEM

College transport
shouldn't feel uncertain.

Students often don't know where their bus is, whether it has already passed their stop or how long they need to wait. Parents can also be left without clear information.

⏱️

Waiting without knowing

Students shouldn't have to stand outside wondering when the bus will arrive.

🚌

Where is my bus?

BusMate is designed around a simple idea: make the bus location easy to understand.

🔔

Better communication

Future alerts can keep students and parents informed about bus movement and status.
